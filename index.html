      Synaptix™ CoS · Capacity of Servers You Own

      Less overhead per workload. More room on hardware you already paid for.

      Traditional virtual machines each carry their own overhead. Synaptix™ CoS uses a lighter way to run workloads, so less of every host goes to virtualization and more goes to the work. It has been demonstrated in lab and data center environments, and validation for wider deployment is still under way.
